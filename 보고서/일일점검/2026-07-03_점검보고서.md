## 발견 버그

### BUG-20260703-01

- **우선순위**: P1 High
- **시나리오**: S01
- **모듈**: 인증
- **제목**: Set-Cookie에 Max-Age=604800 미포함
- **재현 단계**:
  1. `POST /api/auth/login {"username":"teacher1","password":"1234"}`
  2. 응답 헤더 `Set-Cookie` 값 확인
- **기대 결과**: `Set-Cookie: connect.sid=...; Max-Age=604800; HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax`
- **실제 결과**: `Set-Cookie: connect.sid=...; Expires=Fri, 10 Jul 2026; HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax` (Max-Age 없음)
- **HTTP 응답 발췌**:
  ```
  Set-Cookie: connect.sid=...; Path=/; Expires=Fri, 10 Jul 2026 00:38:39 GMT; HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax
  ```
